## Revised hero concept

- `public/images/hero-architecture.webp`: created with the built-in image-generation tool for this website. This is an architectural concept image, not a completed GetDesigned project. The supplied `public/assets/new Get designed logo.png` is used directly, without recolouring or redrawing, in the header and footer and as the site icon.

Generation prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: a cinematic full-bleed website hero background for an Indian bespoke interior design studio. Generate a single premium architectural interior photograph, landscape 16:9, wide-angle editorial view of a spacious warm contemporary living room. Carefully planned double-height proportions, expansive curved ivory plaster wall and subtle cove illumination, rich walnut wall panelling on the left, large floor-to-ceiling windows on the right overlooking soft trees, travertine floor, inviting sculptural cream linen sofa, low substantial dark walnut coffee table, one olive tree and a few restrained ceramic details. Warm natural late-afternoon sunlight, realistic material textures and sophisticated rich brown/cream palette. Camera at human eye level, straight architectural verticals, wide enough to show a complete room with lots of spatial depth. Main architecture and curved ceiling beautiful in upper half. Middle/lower center should have a visually quiet tonal field across the seating to support two lines of large white website typography added later in code. Keep the beautiful photography visible and bright, not pre-darkened. Design feels thoughtful and welcoming, Architectural Digest editorial quality. No text, no logos, no watermark, no people, no web interface, no overly orange colour cast.
